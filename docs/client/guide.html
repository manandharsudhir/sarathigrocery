1. Install the app (APK)

The app is delivered as an APK file (an Android install file), not through the Play Store. You need an Android phone (Android 7 or newer recommended) and an internet connection.

Step 1 — Get the file on the phone

  1. Open the APK file you received (WhatsApp, email, Google Drive or a USB cable).
  2. If it came on WhatsApp/email, tap it to download, then tap it again to open.

Step 2 — Allow installing from this source

  1. Android may say "For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source."
  2. Tap Settings, switch on Allow from this source, then press Back.

Step 3 — Install

  1. Tap Install.
  2. If Google Play Protect shows a warning (common for apps not from the Play Store), tap More details → Install anyway. If it asks to scan the app, you can allow it.
  3. When done, tap Open. The app icon Unique Bakery and Cookies is now on your home screen.
Updating later: when you receive a newer APK, install it the same way over the old one. Your data stays — it is stored safely online, not only on the phone.

2. Create your account and shop

Do this once, as the shop owner.

Sign Up→Verify email→Shop details→Create Shop

Step 1 — Sign up

  1. Open the app. On the login screen tap New shop? Sign up.
  2. Enter your Email, a Password (min 8 characters) and Confirm password.
  3. Tap Sign Up.

Step 2 — Verify your email

  1. Open your email inbox. You will get a message to verify your address (check the Spam folder too).
  2. Tap the link in that email.
  3. Return to the app and tap I've verified. If no email came, tap Resend email.

Step 3 — Set up the shop

  1. Enter Shop name, Your name and Your phone number.
  2. Optional: tick "Start with sample products, customers and suppliers" if you want to practise first. You can edit or remove them later.
  3. Tap Create Shop. You are now logged in as the owner.
If you close the app half-way, just log in again with the same email and password — it continues from where you stopped.

3. Logging in, forgotten password

  1. Enter Email and Password, tap Log In.
  2. Forgot it? Tap Forgot password?, enter your email, tap Send Reset Link. Open the email, set a new password on the page it opens, then log in.
  3. To change your password while logged in: More → Settings → My account → Change Password.
Staff added with a made-up or unused email can't use "Forgot password". The owner can re-add them with another email (see section 12).

4. Getting around the app

The bottom bar is different for each type of user, so each person sees only what they need.

UserBottom tabsHome screen shows
OwnerDashboard · Inventory · Orders · Sales · MoreToday's sales and profit, Needs Attention list, money summary, recent and staff activity.
StaffHome · Orders · Sales · Stock · Customers (or More)Today's sales, a big New Sale button, orders to prepare, low stock items.
DeliveryDeliveries · Collections · AccountOrders assigned to them and the money they collected.
  • More (owner) holds: Customers, Customer Payments, Money, Accounts, Reports, Employees, Settings.
  • The round + button gives quick actions such as New Sale, Collect Payment, Add Expense.
  • Cards on the dashboard can be tapped to open the details behind the number.
  • Needs Attention lists only real problems (low stock, overdue udharo, etc.). An empty list means all is fine.

5. Add your products

  1. Go to Inventory → Add Product.
  2. Fill Product name and optionally SKU / code, brand, description.
  3. Main unit: how you count it, e.g. piece, kg, box, packet.
  4. Other units (optional): tap Add unit, e.g. 1 box = 12 piece. The product can then be sold by piece or by box from the same stock.
  5. Opening stock: how many you have right now, and the cost per unit of that stock.
  6. Min. price per unit: the lowest price it may be sold at. Staff can type a higher price at the counter, not lower.
  7. Low stock level: you get a warning when stock falls to this number.
  8. Tap Save.
You can leave prices blank at first; then the cost is used as the minimum. A product with neither price nor cost can't be sold yet.

6. Add customers

Add regular customers (shops, cafés, homes) to sell on credit (udharo) and take orders. Walk-in customers need no record.

  1. More → Customers → Add Customer (staff: Customers tab).
  2. Enter Shop / customer name, Phone, Location.
  3. Credit limit: maximum udharo allowed. Old udharo (before app): what they already owe you today.
  4. PAN / VAT no. is optional.
  5. Tap Save.

Open a customer to see Outstanding Balance, last payment, Statement, Collect Payment and New sale. The list can be filtered: Owe money, Over limit, No payment 30+ days, Paid up.

7. Make a sale (counter)

  1. Tap Sales → New Sale (or the + button).
  2. Customer: leave as Walk-in customer, or tap the chip at the top to pick / search a customer or add a New customer.
  3. Tap products to add them (each tap adds 1). Use − / + to change quantity. Tap a line to change unit or price.
  4. Tap Charge.
  5. Choose how they pay:
    • Walk-in: pays in full; choose where the money went (cash, bank, wallet).
    • Customer: Paid in full, Udharo (all on credit) or Part paid (type how much they pay now; the rest goes on credit).
  6. Tap Complete sale. Then View bill to share the bill as PDF (WhatsApp etc.).
If a credit sale goes over the customer's credit limit, it is still allowed, but marked Over credit limit and the owner is notified.

8. Customer orders and delivery

Use orders when a customer orders by phone, WhatsApp or in person for later pickup or delivery. Stock is reserved when the order is placed.

Placed→Confirmed→Preparing→Ready→Out for delivery→Delivered

Take an order

  1. Orders → New Order.
  2. Choose the Customer, add Items, choose Delivery (with address) or Pickup, add notes (e.g. "ordered by phone").
  3. Save. Tap View order to share the order slip with the customer if needed.

Move it forward

  1. On each order card or order screen, tap Next: … to move it to the next stage (Confirmed, Preparing, Ready).
  2. If the customer pays in advance: Record payment before dispatch.
  3. When it leaves the shop tap Dispatch & Bill. The bill is created now. Use Print bill (2 copies): one Customer copy, one Shop copy for the customer to sign.

At the customer's door (delivery person)

  1. Delivery person opens Deliveries, picks the order.
  2. Tap Deliver & Collect. Enter the amount received and how (cash, bank, wallet). If signature is turned on, the customer signs on the phone.
  3. If some items were refused, choose "Some items refused" and mark them; stock goes back automatically.
  4. If nothing was delivered (customer away, refused all), use Not delivered and write why.
  5. Back at the shop, hand over the signed shop copy; the owner taps Signed shop copy received.

Delivery staff see their collected money under Collections (Cash to hand over).

9. Collect udharo (credit) payments

  1. Open the customer → Collect Payment (or + → Collect Payment).
  2. Enter the amount and how it was paid. Save.
  3. The balance goes down at once. Payments are applied to the oldest bills first.
  4. To pay one specific bill: open the bill in Sales and tap Collect.
  5. Share a receipt or the full Statement as PDF with the customer.

All payments are listed under More → Customer Payments.

10. Stock adjustments

Stock goes down automatically with sales and deliveries. For anything else (damaged, expired, customer return, return to supplier):

  1. Inventory → open the product → Adjust Stock.
  2. Choose the reason, enter quantity and a Note (required).
  3. Tap Log Adjustment.

Buying stock from suppliers: turn on Suppliers & purchases in Settings → More features. Then More → Purchases records what you bought; it adds stock and sets the cost automatically.

11. Money: expenses and cash

  1. More → Money.
  2. Add expense: amount, category (rent, electricity, salary…), which account it was paid from. Save.
  3. Money in & out: transfers between cash and bank, owner's capital, drawings (money taken for personal use), loans.

The app keeps separate balances for cash, bank and wallet (eSewa, Khalti…). Extra bank/wallet accounts can be added in Settings.

12. Add staff

  1. More → Employees → Add Employee.
  2. Enter Name, Phone, Email (used to log in) and a Temporary password (min 8 characters).
  3. Choose the Role:
    RoleCan do
    StaffSales, orders, customers, stock. Optional toggle "Can handle cash & accounts".
    DeliveryOnly their assigned deliveries and collections.
    Co-ownerEverything the owner can do (business partner).
  4. Save. Give the staff member the APK, their email and the temporary password. They install the app and use Log In (not Sign up), then change their password in Account.
When you add your first staff member, the app offers to turn on safeguards (signature at the door, cash handover, etc.). Recommended if staff handle money. You can change them later in Settings → Safeguards.

Open an employee to change permissions or switch the account off when someone leaves.

13. Settings

More → Settings (owner only):

  • Business details: business name, address, phone (printed on bills), currency, default low-stock level, default margin %.
  • VAT: turn on only if registered; enter PAN/VAT number and rate. Bills become numbered tax invoices.
  • More features: Suppliers & purchases, Categories, Audit log, Expiry tracking. All off by default — turn on only what you need.
  • Safeguards: Signature at the door, Phone check of unsigned payments, Cash handover, Deliveries need internet.
  • My account: change password, switch shop, log out.
  • Danger zone: Export shop data (CSV), reset all data, delete shop and account. These need your password and cannot be undone.

14. Reports and accounts

  • More → Reports: sales, profit, best sellers, stock, customer dues, staff activity, by day / month / fiscal year (Nepali calendar).
  • More → Accounts: Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, and a "books balanced" check. Export CSV/PDF files for your accountant from here.
  • VAT books appear only when VAT is turned on.

15. Fixing mistakes

Entries are never silently deleted. A wrong entry is reversed (with a reason) and entered again, so there is always a clear record.

MistakeHow to fix
Wrong saleOpen the bill in Sales → Fix: correct the items and save. The bill shows "Edited". Staff can fix only today's sales; the owner any.
Some items came backOpen the bill → Fix → Some items came back. Stock and balance adjust.
Wrong paymentOwner opens the payment → reverse it, then record the right one.
Wrong expense / money entryMoney → open the entry → Reverse, then enter again.
Wrong orderBefore dispatch: Cancel Order. After: Not delivered or "Some items refused".

16. Troubleshooting

ProblemSolution
"Install blocked" / can't installAllow "Install unknown apps" for the app you opened the file with (Settings → Apps → that app → Install unknown apps). On Play Protect warning tap More details → Install anyway.
"App not installed"Free some storage space; or uninstall the old version first (your data is online and comes back after login).
Verification email not receivedCheck Spam/Promotions. Tap Resend email. Check the email address is spelled right.
Can't log inCheck email spelling and password (case sensitive). Use Forgot password?.
No internetMost work is saved on the phone and sent when internet returns. Some actions (VAT bills, deliveries with "Deliveries need internet" on, reset) need a connection.
Staff can't see a menuThat role doesn't have permission. Owner: Employees → open the person → change permissions.
Product can't be soldSet a minimum price or cost on the product (Inventory → Edit Product).
